## 3. Neutral Architectural Fallbacks for Missing Packaging

For products where the physical container photo was not included in the source PDF (e.g. Products 23, 28, 29, 34–39), a clean, editorial, vector-embellished placeholder component is generated displaying:
* Standard chemical flask or drum silhouette.
* Precise product name and pharmacopoeial/technical grade badge.
* "Packaging specification available upon quotation request" indicator.
* Never displays a generic or misleading white powder image.
