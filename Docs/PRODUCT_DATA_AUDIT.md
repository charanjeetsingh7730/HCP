## Explicit Product Grade Segregation Rules

To maintain strict compliance with pharmaceutical and industrial procurement standards:
1. **Boric Acid IP vs Technical:** Boric Acid Technical must never be offered or described as interchangeable with Boric Acid IP. Technical grade is designated for ceramics, metallurgy, glass, and agricultural insecticide, whereas IP is for pharmacopoeial buffering and preservation.
2. **Zinc Oxide Segregation:** Three distinct records exist in the HCP catalog:
   - Upper India Zinc Oxide Technical (Rubber, tyre, industrial coatings).
   - Rubamin Zinc Oxide IP (Topical ointments, zinc supplementation).
   - Neelkanth Jodhpur Zinc Oxide IP (Dermatological formulations, cosmetics).
3. **Surfactant Formats:** SLES is cataloged in distinct concentrations:
   - SLES Liquid 28% (Godrej) & SLES 28% (Shree Shyam Group) - Free-flowing clear surfactant liquids.
   - SLES Paste 70% (Godrej) & SLES 70% (Shree Shyam Group) - High-viscosity concentrates requiring warm dilution.
4. **LABSA Variations:**
   - LABSA 90% (Shree Shyam Group) - Standard detergent active intermediate.
   - LABSA 96% (Shree Shyam Group) - High-active low-free-acid grade.
5. **Glycerin Sources:**
   - Glycerin IP/USP (Godrej) - High purity oleochemical glycerin.
   - Glycerin IP (Shree Shyam Group) - Industrial/pharma lot certified.
